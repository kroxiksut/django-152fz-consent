# Consent Module: Scope and Limitations

- [To the consent section](./README.md)
- [To the general documentation section](../README.md)

## What does the module do?

- Manages the life cycle of consents under 152-FZ in the application.
- Provides a verifiable domain model and service layer for a basic consent scenario.
- Supports optional extensions (cookies, API, consent loop, access policies).

## What the module doesn't do

- Does not automatically register the PD operator.
- Does not replace legal examination of documents and processes.
- Does not provide a universal legal engine for different jurisdictions.
- Does not guarantee production-ready integrations with all external verification platforms and vendors.

## Practical conclusion

The package is the technical foundation for the consent process.
Legal, organizational and industry requirements must be supplemented on the project side.
