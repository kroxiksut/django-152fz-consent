## What is demonstrated for cookies

- Cookie banner rendering and user interactions.
- Cookie preferences page (`/cookies/`) and banner actions (`/cookies/banner/`).
- Cookie categories and registry lifecycle through admin.
- Policy and banner revision publishing.
- Runtime behavior with launcher and re-open flow checks.
